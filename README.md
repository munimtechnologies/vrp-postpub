# vrp-postpub (closed before it ran)

This repo was meant to hold a pre-registered test of whether the variance risk premium (Bollerslev, Tauchen and Zhou
2009, *Review of Financial Studies*) still predicts S&P 500 returns after its 1990-2007 sample.

It was closed on 2026-11-17, before any analysis ran, because a prior-art check found the question already
answered: Goyal, Welch and Zafirov, "A Comprehensive 2022 Look at the Empirical Performance of Equity Premium
Prediction" (*Review of Financial Studies* 37(11), 2024), extend the BTZ variance risk premium to 2021. It replicates
strongly in its original window but becomes insignificant once later data is added (their Table 2), changes sign
between the halves of the extended sample, and fails their out-of-sample and market-timing tests. Adding the years
since 2021 would be a small update, not a new result.

MV2's finance research continues in [volvol-term](https://github.com/munimtechnologies/volvol-term), a
pre-registered test of whether volatility is rough all the way down to one day.
